About Our Department

Pioneering Engineering Education, Innovation, and National Development.

The Department of Computer Engineering at Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH), Ogbomoso, is established under the Faculty of Engineering and Technology to provide comprehensive training in computer hardware design, software architecture, embedded systems, microprocessors, and communication networks.

Our curriculum blends rigorous mathematical and physical science foundations with modern hands-on engineering laboratories, preparing students to be technical leaders, inventive founders, and research pioneers globally.

Our Vision

To be a premier center of excellence in computer engineering research, innovation, and technological entrepreneurship in Nigeria and internationally.

Our Mission

To produce high-caliber computer engineers with solid scientific principles, technical ingenuity, and ethical professionalism to resolve societal challenges.

Specialization Tracks

Core Focus Areas & Engineering Domains

Our curriculum provides comprehensive grounding in key technical domains, enabling students to tailor their senior projects and career pathways.

Embedded Systems & IoT

Microcontroller firmware, FreeRTOS, sensor telemetry, ARM architectures, and cyber-physical automation devices.

ARM Cortex ESP32 RTOS KiCad

Software Systems & Cloud

Distributed architectures, operating system kernels, Linux toolchains, microservices, and modern database backbones.

C++ / Rust Docker Linux Kernels PostgreSQL

Networks & Cybersecurity

Enterprise routing, packet filtering, cryptographic protocols, intrusion detection systems, and industrial infrastructure defense.

TCP/IP Cisco IOS Wireshark Firewalls

Digital Logic & VLSI Design

FPGA logic synthesis, Verilog/VHDL simulation, CMOS integrated circuits, and register transfer level (RTL) verification.

Verilog VHDL FPGA RTL

Applied AI & Edge Robotics

Embedded computer vision, TinyML neural inference on microcontrollers, ROS robot kinematics, and autonomous sensors.

TinyML ROS2 OpenCV Jetson

Control & Industrial Automation

Programmable logic controllers (PLCs), PID loop optimization, industrial SCADA architectures, and automated instrumentation.

PLC Ladder SCADA PID MATLAB
